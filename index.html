Block Sush — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026

Overview

Block Sush ("the Game") is a puzzle game developed for Android. This policy explains what information is collected when you play, and how it's used.

Data stored on your device

Your score, coins, achievements, statistics, and settings are stored locally on your device only. This data is never transmitted to us or to any server we control, and we cannot see it.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The Game shows ads through Google AdMob. To serve ads, AdMob may collect device identifiers (such as the advertising ID) and ad-interaction data, and may use this data to personalize ads, subject to your consent where required (EEA/UK). You can review or withdraw ad-personalization consent anytime from Settings → Ad Privacy Options in the app. AdMob's own data practices are described in Google's Privacy Policy.

In-app purchases (Google Play Billing)

The optional "Remove Ads" purchase is processed entirely by Google Play Billing. We never receive or store your payment details — Google handles the transaction and only confirms to us whether it succeeded.

Children's privacy

The Game is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided data through the Game, please contact us so we can address it.

Third-party services

This app uses Google AdMob and Google Play Billing, both governed by Google's own privacy policy and terms.

Your choices

You can manage ad personalization anytime from Settings → Ad Privacy Options in the app, and manage your device's general ad settings from your Android system settings.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Continued use of the Game after changes means you accept the updated policy.
